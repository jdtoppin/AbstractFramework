Noto AP Latin (version 1.000)

This is a Latin/UI-symbol subset of the existing NotoSansCJKsc_AP.ttf hybrid font.
It retains that font's original glyph shapes, advances and vertical metrics.
It is not a replacement made from the separate Accidental_Presidency.ttf.
The full font remains available in AbstractFramework_Media.

Source file:
  OptionalMedia/AbstractFramework_Media/Media/Fonts/NotoSansCJKsc_AP.ttf
Source SHA256:
  55fe9ec3c00308f7d04e9a7c8da2093f54a202097dde1b4a718da617e4d82413
Source internal version:
  Version 2.004;November 1, 2024;FontCreator 15.0.0.2955 64-bit

Reproducible generator: scripts/subset-noto-ap.py
Generator dependency: fonttools==4.60.2 (.utils/requirements-fonts.txt)
Retained Unicode ranges, intersected with the source's available characters:
  U+0020-007E  Printable ASCII
  U+00A0-024F  Latin-1 Supplement and Latin Extended A/B
  U+1E00-1EFF  Latin Extended Additional
  U+2000-206F  General punctuation
  U+20A0-20CF  Currency symbols
  U+2100-214F  Letterlike symbols
  U+2190-21FF  Arrows used by controls and cast previews
  U+2200-22FF  Mathematical operators and comparison symbols
  U+25A0-25FF  Geometric shapes, including status circles/triangles
  U+2700-27BF  Dingbats, including the Mythic+ completion checkmark
  U+FFFD      Replacement character
Required component/alternate glyphs and the original missing-glyph outline
are retained by the subsetter. CJK character mappings are excluded.

The derivative's internal family name is Noto AP Latin and its PostScript
name is NotoAPLatin-Bold to distinguish it from the full font. The original
copyright and OFL license records are preserved in the binary. The original
bundled notice follows verbatim.

-----------------------------------------------------------

The NotoSansCJKsc_AP font is a combination of NotoSansCJKsc and Accidental_Presidency.
NotoSansCJKsc_AP 字体由 NotoSansCJKsc 和 Accidental_Presidency 两种字体组合而成。

NotoSansCJKsc:
    scale: 90%
    voffset: -25
