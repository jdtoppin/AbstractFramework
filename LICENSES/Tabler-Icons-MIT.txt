Tabler Icons v3.46.0
https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons/releases/tag/v3.46.0

The Bag_*, Housing_*, and View_* SVG and TGA assets under Media/Icons are
adapted from the Tabler Icons outline family. The SVG strokes are expanded
into filled cubic paths and normalized for World of Warcraft's Slug renderer;
the TGA files are raster fallbacks generated from those normalized SVGs.
